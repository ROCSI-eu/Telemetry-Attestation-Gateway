## How to read this register

This is an inventory and disposition record, **not a specification**. The sole maintainer prepared it as M1 evidence at solo concept level. No independent discipline approval is claimed; any completed M1 result must be a **solo-maintainer provisional baseline**.

A source reference is line-addressable at the reviewed revision; the owning document named in **Disposition / owner** remains normative. “Duplicate” means deliberately repeated guardrail language whose interpretation is delegated to one owner. “Conflict” includes a historical or apparent incompatibility that could otherwise cause two downstream tests to choose different expectations. “Open decision” identifies a deliberately unselected value and must not be converted into a fixture assumption.

The review covered `docs/delivery-plan.md`, `docs/README.md`, the superseded `docs/system-plan.md`, `docs/product-scope.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/data-and-proof-model.md`, `docs/claim-envelope.md`, `docs/security-and-privacy.md`, `docs/testing-and-operations.md`, `docs/discovery-research-plan.md`, and `docs/decisions.md`. Accepted ADRs were inspected because the documentation-governance rule says they supersede conflicting proposals (`docs/README.md:20-28`) and the decision register makes their current status visible (`docs/decisions.md:32-33`).
